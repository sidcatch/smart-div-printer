## Chrome Web Store requirements

- **Size:** 1280×800 or 640×400 pixels (16:10 ratio). 1280×800 is recommended for sharpness.
- **Format:** PNG or 24-bit JPG (no alpha transparency).
- **Count:** 1 to 5 screenshots. At least 1 is required to publish.
- **Content tips:** no excessive text/marketing overlays; show the actual UI in action.
